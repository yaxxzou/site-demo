PD Dr. Sandra Grinschgl(she/her)

My Name is Sandra Grinschgl (she/her) and I’m a researcher and lecturer at the University of Bern, Switzerland.

My main research areas are:

  • human-technology interaction (esp. cognitive offloading and interaction with artificial intelligence),
  • cognitive enhancement,
  • self-estimation of cognitive abilities/metacognition.

Within my research as well as teaching, I follow Open Science principles such as sharing materials, data, codes, and analyses scripts, preregistering studies, and providing preprints. As a former member of the Graz Open Science Initiative (GOSI) and the Arqus Open Science Ambassadorship Network, I am also experienced in supporting research institutions and individual researchers in the application of Open Science practices. Reladetly, I’m interest in metascientific questions and have collaborated on Big Team Science projects.

Currently, I’m teaching the class “R u Ready: Reproducible Data Preparation and Analyses in R” accompanied by an Ebook: https://r-you-ready.github.io/HS2025/

I’m happy to visit school classes to talk about (my) research. See here for more information: Botschafter/innen-Profile | Young Science

Furthermore, I regularily give workshops on how to use Large Language Models and Artifical Intelligence more broadly for scientific and administrative purposes.
